Privacy Policy

Your privacy is our utmost priority. EduGuard GPS is designed with "Privacy by Design" principles to ensure that student location data is protected through rigorous institutional standards.

security 1. Data Collection

We collect location data solely to verify student attendance and safety. This include GPS coordinates, device identifiers, and timestamped entry/exit logs.

Privacy Guard Notice

"Personal identity data is encrypted at rest using AES-256 standards and never sold to third-party advertisers."

map 2. GPS Usage & Geofencing

Our GPS tracking is **not** constant. It is strictly limited by both location and time parameters to ensure zero surveillance outside of school requirements.

  • check_circle **Defined Boundaries:** Data is only logged when a student enters or exits predefined "Geofences" around school campuses.
  • check_circle **Attendance Windows:** Tracking is only active during scheduled school hours (e.g., 7:00 AM to 4:00 PM).
  • check_circle **Auto-Disable:** Outside of these zones and times, the background location service is dormant.

encrypted 3. Data Security

We utilize SOC2 Type II compliant servers to host all attendance data. Only authorized school administrators with multi-factor authentication (MFA) can access specific student logs.

person_search 4. User Rights

Parents and legal guardians have the right to request a full export of their student's location history at any time. You may also opt-out of GPS tracking, which will require manual check-ins via school staff.

assignment 1. User Responsibilities

By using EduGuard GPS, you agree to provide accurate identification information and ensure the device used for attendance is properly maintained and charged.

Prohibited Actions: Users may not spoof GPS coordinates or use VPNs to circumvent the geofencing requirements.

verified_user 2. Institutional Commitment

The school commits to using this data exclusively for student safety and educational administrative purposes. We will not share individual student patterns with law enforcement without a specific legal warrant or immediate safety threat.

gavel 3. Limitation of Liability

EduGuard is a safety *assistance* tool. While we strive for 100% accuracy, hardware limitations (signal interference, building materials) may affect GPS precision. The school is not liable for data inaccuracies caused by the user's mobile device.
